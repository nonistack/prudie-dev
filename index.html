Questions people ask before booking

Short answers. Longer ones on the call.

How much does it cost to automate lead routing for a small business?
A single lead routing workflow costs $3,500 as a fixed price and takes about a week. A connected system covering intake, routing, follow-up, and reporting costs $8,500 and takes about three weeks. Ongoing monitoring and changes run $1,200 a month.
How long does an automation build take?
One workflow takes about a week from the scoping call to handover. A multi-workflow system takes about three weeks. The main variable is how quickly I get credentials and answers from your side, so builds move fastest when one person owns the project internally.
What is n8n, and why self-hosted?
n8n is an open source workflow automation tool, similar in purpose to Zapier or Make. Self-hosting means the workflows run on a server you own, so pricing stays flat as your volume grows, your customer data stays in your infrastructure, and you keep everything if we stop working together. Hosting typically costs $10 to $40 a month.
What happens when an automation breaks?
Every workflow I build has a failure path. A step that fails is retried on a delay, and if it still fails, an alert goes to a channel you choose with the record attached so someone can finish it by hand. Silent failure is the single most common flaw in automations built by people who have never operated one.
Do I own the workflows you build?
Yes. The workflows, the code, the server, and the credentials are all yours. Everything is exported to a repository you control, and the handover includes a recorded walkthrough plus a written runbook so your team can maintain it.
Which CRMs and tools do you work with?
Anything with an API or a webhook, which covers HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, Airtable, Google Workspace, Slack, Stripe, and most industry-specific tools. Bring your list to the call and I will tell you on the spot which parts are straightforward and which will take real work.
Do you work with businesses outside Los Angeles?
Yes. The work is fully remote across the United States. Calls happen on video, and most clients I have talked to prefer it that way.
