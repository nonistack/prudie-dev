Questions worth answering before you book

Short answers here. We'll get into your specific workflow on the call.

What exactly do you build?

I build the workflows behind lead intake, routing, follow-up, handoffs, notifications, and reporting. If your team is repeatedly moving information from one system to another or following the same set of steps by hand, it's probably worth looking at.

How do I know if my process is worth automating?

If a process happens frequently, follows reasonably consistent rules, and takes people time to execute or monitor, it's a good candidate.

You don't need to know exactly what should be automated before the call. Bring me the process that wastes the most time, and I'll help you identify what makes sense to automate.

How much does it cost?

A single lead-routing workflow is $3,500 fixed price and typically takes about one week.

A connected system covering intake, routing, follow-up, and reporting is $8,500 and typically takes about three weeks.

Ongoing monitoring and changes are $1,200/month if you want me to stay involved after launch.

You'll know the price before any build work starts.

How much of my time will this take?

Usually, less than people expect. I'll need someone on your side who can explain the current process, provide access to the relevant tools, and answer questions when something is unclear.

The biggest projects move fastest when one person owns the project internally and can get me answers without waiting several days.

What if I don't use the same tools you do?

That's usually fine. I work with tools that expose an API or webhook, including HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, Airtable, Google Workspace, Slack, Stripe, and many industry-specific platforms.

Bring your current stack to the call. I'll tell you what's straightforward, what needs custom work, and what I wouldn't automate.

What happens if something breaks?

The system should tell you.

Workflows are built with retries and failure handling. If a step continues to fail, you'll receive an alert with enough information to understand what happened and finish the task manually if needed.

An automation that fails silently isn't automated. It's a liability.

Where does my data live?

The workflows run on infrastructure you own rather than inside a platform that I control. That means you control the server, credentials, workflows, and data moving through them.

We'll also scope access around the systems the workflow actually needs.

Am I locked into n8n or another platform?

No. I use n8n for many builds because it gives small businesses a flexible, self-hosted alternative to per-task automation platforms like Zapier and Make.

The important part is ownership. You own the infrastructure and the workflows, not me.

What do I actually get at the end?

A working automation, your workflows and code, your infrastructure and credentials, plus documentation explaining how the system works.

Handover includes a recorded walkthrough and written runbook so someone on your team can understand and operate it.

What happens after the build?

You can run it yourself, or I can stay involved through ongoing monitoring and changes for $1,200/month.

There is no requirement to stay on a retainer to keep your system running.

Do you work with businesses outside Los Angeles?

Yes. Everything is remote and I work with small businesses across the United States.
