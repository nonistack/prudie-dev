Case studies

Lead routing, from form to CRM to Slack

A submission gets enriched, scored against routing rules, written to the CRM, and announced to the owning rep. Failed steps enter a retry queue, and anything that still fails raises an alert with the payload attached.

Invoice extraction, with a human approval gate

OCR and a local LLM draft every field, six rules check the math, and each field gets a confidence score. Nothing posts to accounting until a person approves it, and the code has no path that skips that step.

Knowledge retrieval, with a measured refusal gate
